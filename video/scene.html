Step 3 · Log

Log your dive

  • 📝Back on your dashboard, find Log a dive.
  • 🔢The dive day is today (UTC). Type your score, 0–700.
  • 🧀Add a brag line if you like. It's optional.
  • 🌊Tap Log dive 🌊 One score per day. Logging the same day again overwrites it.
  • 🔥Log every day before the UTC reset to keep your streak alive.

🤿How scoring works

  • Krillion gives you 7 prompts a day. Rare answers pay more, up to 100 each.
  • So a perfect day is 700 points. Log your total here.
  • Every point is 10 metres of depth. 700 points is 7,000 m down.
  • Streaks count consecutive UTC days with a logged dive.
Game days follow UTC, just like Krillion's daily reset. 1 point = 10 metres of depth.
